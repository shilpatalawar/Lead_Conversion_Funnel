# Lead Conversion Funnel Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing lead data to understand lead generation, conversion performance, and customer engagement. The goal is to identify effective lead sources and provide data-driven recommendations to improve lead conversion.

## 2. Business Problem
Businesses generate leads through multiple sources, but not all leads convert into customers. This project analyzes lead data to identify which sources perform best and where conversion improvements are needed.

## 3. Project Objectives
- Identify the lead sources generating the highest number of leads.
- Calculate and compare conversion rates across lead sources.
- Analyze converted and non-converted leads.
- Understand the relationship between website engagement and lead conversion.
- Identify patterns and opportunities for business improvement.
- Present insights through visualizations and a Power BI dashboard.

## 4. Dataset
- **Dataset:** X Education Lead Scoring Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle – Lead Scoring Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/amritachatterjee09/lead-scoring-dataset)
- **Number of Records:** 9,240
- **Number of Columns:** 37
- **Target Column:** `Converted`

The dataset contains information about lead sources, lead origins, website visits, website engagement, and conversion status.

## 6. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Identified and handled missing values.
- Standardized inconsistent category names.
- Removed unnecessary leading and trailing spaces.
- Checked numerical columns for missing or invalid values.
- Prepared the data for further analysis.

## 7. Exploratory Data Analysis
- Analyzed the distribution of leads across different sources.
- Compared conversion rates by lead source.
- Examined converted and non-converted lead counts.
- Studied website visits and time spent on the website.
- Examined relationships between numerical variables and conversion.
- Identified patterns in lead generation and conversion performance.

## 8. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Leads
- Converted Leads
- Non-Converted Leads
- Overall Conversion Rate
- Total Leads by Lead Source
- Conversion Rate by Lead Source
- Conversion Rate by Lead Origin

## 9. Key Findings
- Google generated the highest number of leads in the analyzed data.
- Reference and Welingak Website showed high conversion rates.
- Website time spent had a positive relationship with lead conversion.
- Lead sources differed in both lead volume and conversion performance.
- Sources with very few leads require further data before drawing reliable conclusions.

## 10. Business Recommendations
- Investigate and maintain lead sources with strong conversion performance.
- Improve follow-up and engagement strategies for low-converting sources.
- Review the conversion process for high-volume sources such as Google.
- Use website engagement as one factor when prioritizing leads.
- Track key performance indicators regularly to support business decisions.
